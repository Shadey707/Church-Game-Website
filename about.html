What is Church Games?

Church Games is a website of quiet games that you can play at church, with or without other people. The games are simple and easy to play in your device.

Why did we make this site?

Sometimes there are quiet moments at church, like before a meeting starts or while waiting for others. We made this site so people have a calm and respectful way to use that time, instead of being bored or distracted.

How does it work?

Go to the Games page, choose a game, and start playing. You don't need to create an account or download anything. You can play alone or share your phone with someone next to you.
